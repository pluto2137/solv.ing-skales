Welcome in Alder for $25 per stay. Please keep them off the beds.
Cancellation
Free up to 14 days before arrival. After that, we refund 50% if we can rebook the dates.
Getting here
The last mile is a gravel road. Any car makes it in summer; in winter, bring chains or let us know and we'll plow early.
