Welcome

A single-story, drive-up motel on the old highway at the edge of town. Clean rooms, quiet nights and coffee on in the lobby from 6 am.

Where you'll stay

Summer rates (June to September) are shown. From October to May every room costs $15 to $25 less, and the 7th night of a weekly stay is free.

Around here

  • Trailheads 15 minutes up the canyon road
  • A trout river 2 miles south
  • Main Street diner and hardware store, a 5-minute walk
  • County fairgrounds, with a rodeo every August

Good to know

Check-in and check-out
Check-in from 3 pm (arriving late? we text you a door code). Check-out by 11 am.
Pets
Dogs welcome in Double Queen rooms, $15 per night, up to two dogs.
Minimum stay
One night. Two nights on fair weekend in August.
What's included
Free Wi-Fi, Coffee in the lobby from 6 am, Truck and trailer parking, Guest laundry, Picnic tables and grills, One EV charger, Ice machine, Fish-cleaning station.
About us
Family-run since 1987, hosted by Dale and Rosa.
