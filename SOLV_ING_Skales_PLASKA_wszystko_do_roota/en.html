How the enquiry system works

  1. Every enquiry in one list. New enquiries from your website form, Instagram, Messenger and booking portals land in one list.
  2. A draft reply straight away. AI drafts a reply using your prices and the way you usually write.
  3. You approve it on your phone. You read the draft and tap approve. Nothing is sent without you.
  4. Follow-up reminders. If the lead goes quiet, you get a reminder with a ready follow-up.
  5. Your calendar, built in. Free and booked dates come from your calendar, so you never double-book.

It all works from your phone, and you keep full control of every message. We built it for our own small business first, and now set it up for others.
