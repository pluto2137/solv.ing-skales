How it works

  1. Audit. A short form or a call. We look at how customers find you, how you deal with them and what takes up most of your time. Free.
  2. Diagnosis. We show you, in numbers, where you lose customers, time and money.
  3. A plan that fits. What to change first, what comes later, and why. No two plans are the same.
  4. Action and results. We put the plan into action, measure it and show you the numbers. You see what works.

Who we work with

Businesses of any kind that want to grow: restaurants, salons, workshops, shops, offices, schools, service and event companies, vacation rentals and larger companies. The industry doesn't matter. We start from your business, not from a ready-made product.

How we work

  • You see it before you pay in full. We show you the finished work and make your changes before you pay the rest.
  • Payment that fits you. Usually half at the start and half after you accept the work. Some projects are paid in stages, or per result, for example per new customer. We agree it with you before we start.
  • Everything stays yours. Domains, accounts and data are in your name. We work with manager access, never your passwords.
  • In plain language. By message or by call, whichever suits you, in English or Polish.
