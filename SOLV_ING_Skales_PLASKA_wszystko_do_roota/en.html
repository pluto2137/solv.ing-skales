Common questions

What does SOLV.ING do?

We help businesses grow. First we get to know the business and find what holds it back. Then we make a plan and put into action what it needs: from customer handling and marketing to automation and custom tools.

How much does it cost?

There's no price list, because every business needs something different. You get a quote after the free audit, before you pay anything.

Is it a one-off project?

It doesn't have to be. We work best as a long-term partner: after launch we keep measuring results, improve what works and plan the next steps together.

How does payment work?

You pay after you approve: before paying in full, you see the finished work. Usually half at the start and half after approval, but we adapt the payment model to the project.

How long does it take?

It depends on the plan. We show first results as soon as we can and roll out bigger changes in stages.

Do you guarantee results?

We don't promise numbers out of thin air. We record your numbers at the start and compare them with you after the work is live.

Who do you work with?

Businesses of any industry that want to grow, from one-person businesses to larger companies, in Europe and the United States.

Who owns the work?

You do. Domains and accounts are in your name, and once it's paid for, everything we made is yours.
