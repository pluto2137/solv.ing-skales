Welcome

All Seasons Resort is a family resort of eight cabins on Woman Lake in Longville, Minnesota, owned since 2020 by Dennis and Angelia Sawyers, who previously ran a campground in Nebraska for over ten years. Cabins come with kitchens, linens and a free dock space, and three cabins stay open year-round for winter guests.

Where you'll stay

2026 peak season June 13 – August 15 (Saturday to Saturday weekly rentals); off-peak May 1 – June 13 and August 15 – October 1 at 15% off weekly and 10% off daily rates. Rates are for 2 people; extra guests $35/day or $150/week. Winter (Oct 1 – May 1) cabins 6, 7 and 8 only – call for rates. $400 deposit per cabin week.
