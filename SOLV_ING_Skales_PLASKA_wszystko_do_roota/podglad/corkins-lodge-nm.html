Welcome

Corkins Lodge is a collection of 10 log and wooden cabins in the mountains near Chama, with views of the Brazos Cliffs and dark skies for stargazing. For a century it has welcomed nature lovers to 700+ acres of hiking and 2.5 miles of private Brazos River access.

Where you'll stay

Open summer, fall and spring. Rates include 2–4 people per cabin; additional persons $25 each per night.
