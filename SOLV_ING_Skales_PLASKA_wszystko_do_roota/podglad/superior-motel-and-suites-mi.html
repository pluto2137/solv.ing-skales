Welcome

Superior Motel & Suites is in downtown Munising, close to Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore, Grand Island and 17 Alger County waterfalls. Its 15 rooms and 3 suites have rustic, wildlife-themed U.P. decor, and in winter guests can snowmobile right from the property.

Where you'll stay

Open year-round; some suites have a 2-night minimum. No rates published.
