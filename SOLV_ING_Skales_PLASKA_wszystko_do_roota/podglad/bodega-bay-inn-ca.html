Welcome

Bodega Bay Inn is a family-operated boutique inn on the Sonoma Coast, open since 1983 and originally built as an art gallery that still features local artists. It sits on grounds with expansive gardens 300 yards above Bodega Bay, right off scenic Highway 1, with Michelin-starred Terrapin Creek and Roadhouse Coffee next door. The bay is about a 5-minute walk away.
