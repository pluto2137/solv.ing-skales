Welcome

La Doña Luz Inn is an historic bed & breakfast in a 200-year-old adobe, parts of which date to 1802, adjoining the original home of La Doña Luz Lucero de Martinez. Innkeeper Paul "Paco" Castillo has restored and added to the inn, which displays original fine art and authentic Native American and Spanish Colonial antiques throughout. It is a few steps from the old Taos plaza and across the street from the Kit Carson House.
