Welcome

Simply Serene Cabin Rentals offers cozy, pet-friendly cabins in the mountains of Cosby, Tennessee, close to the Cosby, Greenbrier and Big Creek entrances of Great Smoky Mountains National Park. Each cabin sleeps four and comes with a private hot tub, a fireplace and a fully equipped kitchen, with Gatlinburg and Pigeon Forge a short drive away.

Where you'll stay

Winter (Dec–Feb) $129–$149/night; Spring $149–$159; Summer $169–$189; Fall $149–$179; holiday rates higher. Cleaning fee $165 per stay; pet fee $85.
