Welcome

Crystal Cove Resort sits right on Norfork Lake, about 10 miles east of Mountain Home, Arkansas, a fishing spot known for bass, walleye, striper and crappie. It offers nine housekeeping cabins with fully equipped kitchens and screened-in porches, spaced out among red oak, dogwood and cedar trees, with its own private boat and swim dock.

Where you'll stay

Spring & fall: weekly or daily rates with a 2-night minimum; summer: weekly only, Saturday to Saturday; Memorial and Labor Day nightly rates. Extra persons $65/week or $20/day. $150 deposit per week reserved.
