Welcome

3Gs Resort is a quiet cabin resort among pines and birch on Lake George, just five miles from downtown Rhinelander, Wisconsin. Cabins have fully equipped kitchens, fireplaces, decks or screen porches, air conditioning and free Wi-Fi, with a rowboat included. Your hosts are Connie Brown (Cabin 1) and Mary Kircher (Cabins 5 and 9).

Where you'll stay

2027 rates, plus 9% state and local tax. Off-peak weeks from $1,100 (Windsong #9) and $1,225 (Edgewood #1); peak weeks $1,600–$1,975. Daily rentals have a 3-night minimum (Cabins 1 and 9). Deposit $300 per week ($100 for shorter stays), non-refundable.
