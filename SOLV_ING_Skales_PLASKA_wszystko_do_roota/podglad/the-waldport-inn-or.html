Welcome

The Waldport Inn, formerly the American Inn & Suites, got its start in 2016 when Paul & Terry Lopez took on the building after it had sat vacant for over four years. The newly remodeled motel is within walking distance of the beach and restaurants, with spacious family suites and king and queen rooms. Newport is only about 20 minutes away.
