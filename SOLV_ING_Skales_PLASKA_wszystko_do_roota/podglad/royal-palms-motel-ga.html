Welcome

The Royal Palms is Tybee Island's retro roadside marvel: a casual mid-century surf motel about 500 feet from the beach, with a large pool and a sun-drenched pool deck. It first opened in 1961, and restaurants, shopping and island entertainment are a short walk away.
