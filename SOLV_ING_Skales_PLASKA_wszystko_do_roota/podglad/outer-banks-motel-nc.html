Welcome

Outer Banks Motel is an oceanfront motel on Hatteras Island in Buxton, steps from the sandy beach with Pamlico Sound just across the street. Established by the Dillon family in 1955, it offers motel rooms and oceanfront cottages on site, plus more than a dozen Village Cottages about a mile away in Buxton.
