Welcome

Schroon Lake Motel and Cabins is a family-owned lakefront motel with weekly cabin rentals on the 9 miles of Schroon Lake in the Adirondacks. The family has been coming to Schroon Lake since the 1960s, and two generations have worked together to restore the property.

Where you'll stay

Prices plus tax. Cabins rent weekly, Saturday to Saturday; nightly cabin stays ($225-$250) in May, June and September with a 2-night minimum. Holiday dates may carry higher rates.
