Welcome

Built in 1884, The Barrington Inn is a farmhouse on a hilltop overlooking New Harbor and Great Salt Pond on Block Island. It offers nightly rooms with private baths, two barn apartments, and weekly cottages at its sister property, the Harbourview Guest House on Corn Neck Road.

Where you'll stay

Now booking the 2026 and 2027 seasons; 3-night minimum for stays including a Saturday in July or August. Harbourview cottages rent weekly, Saturday to Saturday, May 9 to October 13 (2025 dates).
