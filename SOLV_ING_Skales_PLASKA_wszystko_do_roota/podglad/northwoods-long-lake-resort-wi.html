Welcome

Northwoods Long Lake Resort sits beneath old-growth firs in the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest, on 330-acre Long Lake. A getaway for families and outdoor lovers since the 1930s, it offers rustic housekeeping cabins near a private shoreline, dock and fire pit. Hosts Joe and Lisa have spent over 40 years making memories at Long Lake.
