Welcome

Petunia Cottage (Days Cottage #17) was built in 1931 and sits at Beach Point in North Truro, on a Cape Cod Bay sand beach that is private for the group of cottages. It has been upgraded with larger beds and a gas fireplace while keeping its historical charm, and rents by the week, Saturday to Saturday.

Where you'll stay

Summer weeks are $3,400 per week plus hotel tax; rental week is Saturday to Saturday. Openings for the 2027 season include weeks in May, June and October.
