Welcome

Trouthaven offers ten modernized vintage knotty-pine cabins on the quiet side of Wallowa Lake, each with a covered porch and a lake view. Walt and Carol Hearne built the lodge themselves after buying half a mile of lakefront, and the family has kept the tradition going for more than 60 years.
