Welcome

Family-owned lakefront cottages on Otter Lake, part of the Eagle River Chain of Lakes, about 3 miles from downtown Eagle River. The Lumley family has run the resort since 1982, with a private sandy beach and free boats, kayaks and paddleboards for guests.

Where you'll stay

Summer is rented by the week, Saturday to Saturday, from the second week of June to the last week of August (tax not included). Off-season nightly rates from $160 with a 2-night minimum; Memorial Day and Labor Day weekends 3 nights.
