Welcome

Golden Bear Cottages sits off Big Bear Boulevard, across from hiking and biking trails, about a mile from downtown Big Bear and five miles from Snow Summit. Cottages range from Jacuzzi studios for two to large group cabins, most with wood-burning fireplaces, full kitchens and patios with BBQs.
