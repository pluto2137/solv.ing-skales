Welcome

The Forge Motel is in the heart of Old Forge, a gateway town to New York's Adirondack Park, with rooms overlooking the start of the Fulton Chain of Lakes. Every stay includes parking, breakfast and Wi-Fi, and a heated indoor pool is open year-round.

Where you'll stay

Rates from $95/night off-season to $200/night in peak season, depending on room type; based on double occupancy and include parking, breakfast and Wi-Fi.
