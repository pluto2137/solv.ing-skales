Welcome

Cliff Cottage Inn offers seven unique suites in four beautifully restored historic buildings in downtown Eureka Springs, just 17 steps from Main Street. The suites are named after writers and artists, feature jacuzzi tubs (one has a hot tub), and a hot breakfast can be delivered to your suite. Innkeeper Justin Robins has run the inn full-time for nine years and has renovated every building.
