Welcome

Twin Ponds Cottage is a two-bedroom, four-season lakeside cottage on Twin Ponds in Brookfield, Vermont, run by L.H. Stowell & Son. Guests come for fishing, swimming, boating, hiking, local Vermont attractions or plain relaxation, and two boats are available for their use. It suits couples and small groups of five or fewer, including remote workers who need reliable internet.

Where you'll stay

2026 rates (double occupancy): 4 days/3 nights $675; 1 week $1,200; 2+ weeks $1,000/week; 4 weeks $3,750; more than 28 nights (May–October) $130/night. Additional occupants $30 per person per night (up to five guests total). $100 refundable damage deposit at check-in; Vermont 9% rooms tax + 3% short-term rental surcharge for stays under 31 nights. Weeks usually run Saturday to Saturday.
