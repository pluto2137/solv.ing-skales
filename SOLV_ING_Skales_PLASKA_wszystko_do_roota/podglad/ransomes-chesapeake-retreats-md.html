Welcome

Ransome's Chesapeake Retreats is a 250-acre waterfront estate on the Chesapeake Bay, about 15 minutes southwest of Cambridge on Maryland's Eastern Shore. Three homes with 12+ bedrooms and 8.5 baths sleep 35–40 guests, for family reunions, retreats and weddings; five generations of the Ransome family have enjoyed the property.
