Welcome

A family resort on spring-fed Howard Lake in the Chippewa National Forest that has been a tradition for vacationers for over 107 years. Ten newly remodeled cabins, a lodge with pizza and ice cream, a sandy beach and 1,100 feet of shoreline, plus camping.
