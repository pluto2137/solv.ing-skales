Welcome

Built by the Baltich family in 1939, Northwind Lodge offers lakeside cabins on Jasper Lake near Ely, at the gateway to the Boundary Waters Canoe Area Wilderness. Stays include complimentary pontoon boat time plus kayaks, canoes and SUPs.

Where you'll stay

Summer June 21 – Sept 19; Spring/Fall May 15 – June 20 and Sept 20 – Oct 12. 3-night, 4-night and weekly packages; $300 non-refundable deposit.
