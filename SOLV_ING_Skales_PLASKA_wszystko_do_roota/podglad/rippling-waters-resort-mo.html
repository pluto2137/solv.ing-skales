Welcome

Rippling Waters Resort is a family-owned and operated resort on the quiet Niangua arm of the Lake of the Ozarks, about one mile by water from Ha Ha Tonka State Park. It has served families for 60 years with 15 lakeside units, from a rustic 1950 one-bedroom cabin to a three-bedroom lodge home.
