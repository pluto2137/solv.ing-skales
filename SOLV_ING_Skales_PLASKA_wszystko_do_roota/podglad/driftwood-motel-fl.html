Welcome

Family owned and operated since 1958, the Driftwood Motel and cottages sit across from the Indian River in Jensen Beach. Units range from efficiencies and one-bedroom apartments to cottages, a honeymoon suite and a two-bedroom house, with weekly stays and pet-friendly rooms.
