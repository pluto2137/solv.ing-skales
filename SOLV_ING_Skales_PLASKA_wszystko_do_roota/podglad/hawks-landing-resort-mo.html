Welcome

Hawks Landing Resort in Camdenton is a family-owned resort on the Lake of the Ozarks, originally built in 1964 as The Flame Resort. After sitting vacant, it was bought by David, Karla and Dustin Dailing in 2015, renovated, and reopened in 2016. All cabins are waterfront and, unlike most resorts on the lake, it is open year-round.

Where you'll stay

Rates and minimum stays vary between Summer and Off-Season; cabins are priced for 4 guests, additional guests $15 per person per night.
