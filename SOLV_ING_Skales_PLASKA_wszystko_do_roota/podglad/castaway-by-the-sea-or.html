Welcome

A locally owned cliff-top hotel in Port Orford overlooking the harbor and fishing dock, with an ocean view from every room. Its 13 rooms range from standard queens to two-story townhouses, and pets are welcome.

Where you'll stay

Rates change with the seasons (higher in summer, lower in winter), based on double occupancy; extra adult $20, child $10. 10% off for AAA, AARP, military, veterans and first responders.
