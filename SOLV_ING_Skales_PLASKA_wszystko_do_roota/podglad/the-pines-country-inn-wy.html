Welcome

The Pines Country Inn is a family-owned inn at the edge of the Black Hills, in a quiet hillside neighborhood a few minutes' walk from historic downtown Newcastle. Jins and Rachel bought the historic Pines Motel in 2016 and renamed it in 2019, keeping its reputation for spotless rooms and friendly hospitality.
