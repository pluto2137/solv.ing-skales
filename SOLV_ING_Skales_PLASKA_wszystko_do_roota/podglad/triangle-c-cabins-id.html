Welcome

Triangle C Cabins are standalone log cabins in Stanley, Idaho, built in a rustic style by a father and son in the 1970s. The 2.5-acre property has old-fashioned yard games and porches facing the sunset over the Sawtooth Mountains: a quiet, simple place to rest after a day outdoors.
