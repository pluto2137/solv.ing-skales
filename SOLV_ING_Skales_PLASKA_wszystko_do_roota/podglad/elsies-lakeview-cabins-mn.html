Welcome

Elsie's Lakeview Cabins sit along a quiet stretch of Lake Superior just outside Grand Marais, Minnesota, and have been family-owned for nearly a century. Each cabin is steps from the water, with a private fire pit, full kitchen and its own outdoor space.
