Welcome

Westwood Cottages offers 10 waterfront cottages on 50 acres with about 1,000 feet of private shoreline on Woods Pond in Bridgton, Maine. Started by Fernando and Gladys Potter in 1950, it is now a third-generation family tradition run by Jamie and Sarahjo.
