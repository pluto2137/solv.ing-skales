Welcome

Smokies Bed and Breakfast is a romantic escape in the heart of the Smoky Mountains, with private quarters, featherbeds, fireplaces and jetted tubs. Breakfast is prepared by a chef and can be served in bed, in the garden or fireside in the living room, with dinner available by reservation. The inn can also plan a picnic to a waterfall in Great Smoky Mountains National Park.

Where you'll stay

B&B room rates include a full breakfast; cabin stays do not include meals.
