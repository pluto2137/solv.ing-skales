Welcome

The Chuckwagon Inn Bed & Breakfast sits on 5½ acres, a short three-minute drive from historic Main Street in Fredericksburg, Texas. Its stone house dates to 1854, built by the Tatsch family as a dog-trot structure, and the guest houses offer king-size beds, fireplaces, fully equipped kitchens and views of the Texas Hill Country. Acclaimed chef Becky Higgins, who runs the inn with her daughter Hillary, serves a made-from-scratch breakfast hot each morning.
