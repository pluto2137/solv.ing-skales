Welcome

The Red Brick Inn, also known as The Canton Hotel, was built by Abraham Boyd around 1798–1804 and served as a steamboat stop on the Cumberland River. Guests over the years have included James K. Polk, the Marquis de Lafayette and Jenny Lind. Today it is a bed and breakfast with five rooms and a whole-house option near Lake Barkley.
