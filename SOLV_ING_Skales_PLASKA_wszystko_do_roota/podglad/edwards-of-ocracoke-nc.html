Welcome

Edwards of Ocracoke offers classic island cottages in two locations in Ocracoke Village: a cottage complex of attached units with screened porches and full kitchens, and three historic Duck Cottages listed on the Ocracoke National Register. All cottages are non-smoking and pets are not allowed.
