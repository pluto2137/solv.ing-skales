Welcome

First Farm Inn is a farmhouse bed and breakfast in Northern Kentucky's rolling countryside, about 20 minutes from downtown Cincinnati, in a home dating to the 1870s. Hosts Jen Warner and Dana Kisor serve a homemade all-you-can-eat breakfast, and guests can ride the farm's friendly horses.
