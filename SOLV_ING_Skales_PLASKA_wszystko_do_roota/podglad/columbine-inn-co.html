Welcome

Nestled in the heart of Idaho Springs in a rustic Rocky Mountain setting, Columbine Inn features 16 rooms and suites, some with kitchenettes, on well-manicured grounds in a historic mining town. Rooms have radiant floor heat in winter and air conditioning in summer.

Where you'll stay

Seasonal ranges: rooms $89–$105, king suite $99–$110, cottage $155–$175, luxury cottage $145–$190. Holiday reservations require non-refundable prepayment.
