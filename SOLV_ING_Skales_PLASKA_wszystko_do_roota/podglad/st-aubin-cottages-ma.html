Welcome

St. Aubin's Bayside Cottages are six family cottages on Cape Cod Bay in North Eastham, footsteps from the beach. The business was started in 1955 by Marian and Armand St. Aubin and is carried on by their daughter Lu.

Where you'll stay

Open mid-April through October; weekly rentals in season, partial weeks and weekends in the off-season only. 2026 weekly availability grid lists weeks from $1,400 to $2,400. 1/2 deposit at booking, balance on arrival; MA short-term rental tax 12.45%.
