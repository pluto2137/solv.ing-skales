Welcome

The Bella Vista ("Beautiful View") is a waterfront motel on the shore of Lake Superior in Copper Harbor, with lakeshore rooms and cottages spread across three buildings. Local eateries, trails, gift shops and the Isle Royale Queen dock are all within walking distance.
