Welcome

The Sheffield House is an 1888 Victorian built for Dr. J.E. Bennett, up High Street in Block Island's Historic District, a short walk from town and the beaches. Ed and Diane Hayde have owned and run it since June 2005, with a wrap-around front porch and gardens.

Where you'll stay

Shoulder season May 22–June 11 & Sept 7–27, high season June 12–Sept 6, July 4th week 3-night minimum, quiet season Oct 2–12 weekends only ($210/night). Open May through October.
