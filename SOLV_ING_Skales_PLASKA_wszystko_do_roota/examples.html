What could your site look like? Here are three examples.

Every client gets a free preview built around their own business, with their rooms or services, their rates and their contact details, before paying anything.

Those previews are private. Each one is made for one owner and shared only with them, so we can't show them here. Instead, here are demo sites for made-up businesses, built the same way.

Cabin rental

Fernwood Cabins

Three cabins on a quiet lake, with the rates for every season on one page. Made for owners who want past guests to book them directly instead of through an app.

What to notice

  • Seasonal rates. Switch between summer, spring & fall and winter at the top. The rate table highlights that season, and the sky changes with it.
  • The "Ask for your dates" form. Pick arrival and departure dates and a cabin, and the total for the stay, cleaning fee included, appears before the guest sends anything.
  • On a phone. The rate table turns into one row per cabin, so nobody has to scroll sideways to find a price.

Roadside motel

Juniper Ridge Motel

A family-run, drive-up motel in a small Montana town. Four room types, their rates, the house rules and what's nearby, all on a single page.

What to notice

  • Seasonal rates. Each room card shows its summer rate, and one line explains the lower October to May rates and the free 7th night.
  • The "Ask for your dates" form. Guests pick dates, a room type and the number of guests. The request goes to the owner's inbox, not to a booking platform.
  • On a phone. The call button sits right under the motel's name, and the room cards stack into one easy column.

Open the Juniper Ridge Motel demo →

Dog groomer

Bramble & Biscuit Grooming

A small grooming studio with every service and its price listed. Customers send an appointment request instead of playing phone tag.

What to notice

  • Clear prices. Every service shows a price or a "from" price, so customers know what to expect before they call.
  • The request form. Customers pick a service and a preferred day, and the shop confirms a time by text or email.
  • On a phone. The phone number stays at the top of the page, one tap to call, and the form is easy to fill in with one thumb.
